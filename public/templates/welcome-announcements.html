★ Welcome & safety brief

Welcome to the Sunset Duckies Cup! 🦆🌅

Read aloud at 13:30 — gather everyone on the sand · ~3 minutes · loud, warm, no rushing the safety bits.

"Ducklings, parents, friends — welcome to the very first Sunset Duckies Cup! Before we paddle out, gather in, listen up — a few important things, then we surf."

🎉 First competition we've ever run

This is the first comp the club has ever organised — bear with us if the running order wobbles a little. The waves call the shots, not the schedule. Be patient, be loud for every kid, and we'll all have a great afternoon.

🧭 Where to find things

🍔 Food & water

On the tables — help yourselves any time. The BBQ is going all afternoon: sausages, baguettes, fruit. Drink water, it's a long day in the sun.

🕒 Timetable

Pinned at the registration desk and on the heat board. If you're not sure when your kid is up — check the board first, then ask a helper.

🦆 Helpers in Duckies shirts

Anyone in a Sunset Duckies shirt is a helper. Lost? Confused? Need a leash, a snack, a band-aid? Stop one and ask.

🩹 First aid

First-aid kit is at the registration desk and a trained adult is on site all afternoon. Grazes, jellyfish, wax-in-eye — find a helper immediately.

🚩 Water safety — listen carefully

These rules exist so no one gets hurt. They're not negotiable — if a helper or the head judge calls you back, come back, no arguments.

1
YOU MUST surf WITHIN the poles.
Two flags / poles mark the competition zone. Stay between them. That's where the judges can see you and where we can keep you safe.
2
YOU CANNOT go OUTSIDE the poles.
If you drift past a pole — paddle around the outside and re-enter from behind. Never cut back through the line-up — that's how boards meet heads. Always paddle around.
3
ONE SURFER PER WAVE. 🚫🚫
If someone is already up and riding, the wave is theirs. Don't drop in. Wait, smile, take the next one — there are plenty of waves.
4
Leash on. Helmet if you have one. Sunscreen on.
Board hits hurt. A leash keeps your board with you, not flying into someone else.

⏱️ How a heat runs

We'll call every step from the beach with the mic — you don't have to remember the clock, just listen.

"Three minutes left — still time for one more!"
10:00
🔴 Heat STOPS
"Time! Paddle in — well done!" Catch the wave you're on, then come in.

Heats are 10 minutes. When time's up, finish the wave you're on if you're already riding it — then paddle straight in. Don't start a new wave after the horn.

🏅 Judging — only your TOP 2 waves count

Catch as many waves as you want — but at the end of the heat only your best 2 waves go toward your score. That means it's better to commit hard to a few good ones than to flap on every ripple. Pick your wave, paddle with everything, stand tall.

Each wave is scored on 5 simple things — the judge ticks a box for each one you nail. Max 5 ticks per wave, max 10 across your best 2.

🏊 Paddle & commit
You went for the wave with everything — no hesitation, head down, full strokes.
🚀 Pop-up
Clean push to your feet — hands on the board, jump up, no knees first.
🧘 Stance
Feet apart, knees bent, eyes up the wave — like your coach showed you.
🌊 Ride
You stayed up and rode the wave — even a few seconds counts. Longer = better.
🎉 Stoke
Big smile, claim it, kick out with style. Energy in the water matters!

📣 One last thing — cheer for EVERYONE

Parents, friends, fellow ducklings — cheer for every kid, not just yours. The kid who falls hardest gets the loudest cheer. The kid who's scared and paddles out anyway is a hero. That's the Duckies way.
