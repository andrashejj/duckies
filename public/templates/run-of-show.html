Notes for the day

  • Surf check by 10:00 — confirm go/no-go. If surf is too big, drop to Duckling-only and shorten heats — Andras makes the call on the morning.
  • Mid-event break matters. 4.5 hours is long for little kids — food, dry layer, unstructured play, or the second half falls apart.
  • Water marshals rotate every hour. Bench the burnt-out marshal during the break.
  • Photographer covers every heat. One hero shot per kid is the goal.
  • Bonfire from 18:00. Someone owns the firewood run; keep the fire well back from boards and bags, a water bucket within reach, and douse it fully before the last family leaves. Sunset is ~17:50 — light it as the awards wrap.
