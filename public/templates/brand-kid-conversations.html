Kid conversations run sheet 🏄

Phase 3 · Community · for the coaches at Monday and Friday training

The job this week is mostly to watch and write down exact words. Have the three direction sheets on a phone to pass around at Monday training, put the printed stickers on the table, and let the kids come to them. Say this out loud at the start:

“We're picking how the club looks. It's a conversation, not a vote. Your job is to be honest.”

1. The sticker test · Monday to Friday

Nobody has to take one. Count what actually happens, without nudging.

2. The embarrassment question · during the week

“Which one would embarrass you if it was on your rashie?”

Ask this instead of “which do you like”; it produces more honest answers. Record the two age groups separately.

3. The memory test · Friday

“You saw the three designs on Monday. What do you remember?”

4. The drawing test · optional, if there's time

One kid describes a badge to another kid, who draws it without seeing it. Whatever makes it through the description is the part that actually works.
