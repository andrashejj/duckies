★ Sunset Duckies · Club Membership

Join the club 🦆

Tamarin Bay · Mondays & Fridays · 4–6pm

Paper fallback for the private registration link. Membership is Rs 3,000 for one child or Rs 5,000 for a family, per semester, paid before registration. For a family membership, complete one form for each child.

What membership covers

  • Club training — Mondays & Fridays, 4–6pm in Tamarin Bay. You choose one or two sessions a week.
  • Nothing else is included. Gear is your own (no club rashie), and competitions like the Sunset Duckies Cup, socials and other events are organised and ticketed separately.
  • Minimum age 7, confident open-water swimmers only, and one parent or guardian in the water every session.
