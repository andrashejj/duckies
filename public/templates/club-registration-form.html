★ Sunset Duckies · Club Membership

Join the club 🦆

Tamarin Bay · Mondays & Fridays · 4–6pm

Your kid wants in? Fill this in and send it back to Andras by WhatsApp or email. Membership is Rs 3,000 for one child or Rs 5,000 for a family, per semester. For a family membership, complete one form for each child.

What membership comes with

  • One free first session for every new duckie.
  • Custom club rashie — printed with your kid's name, theirs to keep.
  • Two coached sessions a week — Mondays & Fridays in Tamarin Bay.
  • Safety cover at trainings — first-aid kit and coaches on the beach.
  • Club WhatsApp — surf calls, session updates, recap photos.
  • End-of-season certificate and a spot in any club socials and BBQs.
  • Competitions like the Sunset Duckies Cup are ticketed separately — not included.
