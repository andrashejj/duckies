Running the draw:
  • Seeding: siblings are already split across heats and ages kept even — check it still holds if anyone scratches or swaps division.
  • One combined Final, up to 8 surfers (2–3 ducklings + 3–4 ducks):
    • Ducks (3–4 advance): top boy + top girl from each Duck heat = 4. Fallback: if a heat has no boys or no girls, advance the next-best so each crown is still fillable.
    • Ducklings (2–3 advance): top scorer from each Duckling heat = 2. Head judge can add 1 wildcard if a third duckling clearly earns it.
    • The head judge marks the advancing surfers on the scorecards at the end of each qualifier.
  • Crowns are decided inside the Final: rank ducklings vs ducks separately on the same scorecard. Top duckling boy + top duckling girl = Duckling King & Queen; top duck boy + top duck girl = Duck King & Queen.
  • If surf is too big: drop to Duckling-only and shorten heats — same draw, fewer rounds.
