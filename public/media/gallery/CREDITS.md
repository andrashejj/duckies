# Gallery

Photos and clips on `/gallery`. Originals are in `.local-data/whatsapp/sunset-duckies/` (not committed; `manifest.tsv` has the WhatsApp source file for each), derivatives here are WebP (1600 and 800 long edge) and 960px H.264 clips. Entries in `src/data/gallery.ts` carry the photographer and date per item — "a club parent" is the group member with no name saved in Andras's contacts.

Media consent is recorded on each kid's signed waiver; the photos that show recognisable kids are `board-lineup-sunset`, `crew-and-boards`, `boards-under-the-mountain`, `bonfire-faces`, `bonfire-crew` and `heart-cake`.

The `le-morne-*` and `reef-foam-from-above` stills and the two `le-morne-*` clips are frames/cuts from the club's 4K drone footage of 12 July 2026 (Nextcloud share `wtLrqrgfGCi5w6i`; originals in `.local-data/nextcloud-drone/`). The adults in them are club parents; `le-morne-sandcastles` shows two Duckies from far above.
