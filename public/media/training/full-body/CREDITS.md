# Full-body workout stills

Source: [40 Min FULL BODY Workout (No Equipment)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cc9Eelzd6wQ), by **Dan the HIIT Man / TIFFxDAN**.

These images are frames extracted from that video for the accompanying exercise reference cards. They are not Sunset Duckies photographs or generated illustrations. Original video and image rights remain with the creator; attribution does not imply a licence or endorsement.

Each image filename matches an exercise ID in `src/data/full-body-workout.ts`. The `frame` field records the exact extraction time in seconds; `start` records the exercise’s work interval. Source URL and frame time are also embedded in each WebP’s EXIF ImageDescription. Frames retain the full video composition and are encoded at 1280 × 720.

The warm-up and main-workout titles were checked against the video’s on-screen labels. The six cool-down moves have no on-screen titles; the cards use descriptive names. Timings follow the visible countdowns, including the 20-second transition after warm-up, the 30-second rest after squat jumps, the 30-second transition to the finisher, and the 20-second recovery after the final Tabata interval.
