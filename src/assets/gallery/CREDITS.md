# Gallery

Photos and clips on `/gallery`, members-only. This folder is bundled into the server function and served through `/gallery/asset/…` behind the club sign-in — nothing here is a public URL. The public Cup and home pages use separate 480px copies in `public/media/thumbs/` (the `photo:` slugs in `src/data/site.ts`). Originals are in `.local-data/whatsapp/sunset-duckies/` (not committed; `manifest.tsv` has the WhatsApp source file for each), derivatives here are WebP (1600 and 800 long edge) and 960px H.264 clips. Entries in `src/data/gallery.ts` carry the photographer and date per item — "a club parent" is the group member with no name saved in Andras's contacts.

Media consent is recorded on each kid's signed waiver; the photos that show recognisable kids are `board-lineup-sunset`, `crew-and-boards`, `boards-under-the-mountain`, `bonfire-faces`, `bonfire-crew` and `heart-cake`.

The `le-morne-*` and `reef-foam-from-above` stills and the two `le-morne-*` clips are frames/cuts from the club's 4K drone footage of 12 July 2026 (Nextcloud share `wtLrqrgfGCi5w6i`; originals in `.local-data/nextcloud-drone/`). The adults in them are club parents; `le-morne-sandcastles` shows two Duckies from far above.

The `cup-*` stills are from the 1,178-photo Cup Vol. 01 album (31 May 2026) in Nextcloud share `Doz36QepJBNrmfc` (originals for the picks in `.local-data/nextcloud-cup/`); the same share's `20260604 Little Reef Surfing` folder is the source of the `little-reef-*` stills and clips (`.local-data/nextcloud-reef/`). Faces are visible in several Cup photos — `cup-the-crew`, `cup-finalists`, `cup-finalists-2`, `cup-arms-out`, `cup-beach-sprint` — check media consent on the waivers. The Cup page heroes (`public/media/cup/`) are photos 1334 and 1192 from the same album.
