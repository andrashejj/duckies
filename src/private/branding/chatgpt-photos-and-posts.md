## House style (paste first)

> For every image in this chat: a real documentary photograph, not an illustration, painting, 3D render or poster. Shot on a 35mm full-frame camera with a 35–50mm lens, natural light only, true-to-life colours, fine film grain, believable water, sand and skin. West coast of Mauritius. Children's faces are turned away from the camera, hidden by spray or too distant to recognise. Plain rash vests in faded ochre, teal or coral, no brands. No lettering, logos, watermarks, borders or graphic effects.
>
> Two locations. **Tamarin Bay**: a wide sandy bay with gentle beach-break waves, casuarina (filao) trees along the shore, the Rempart and Trois Mamelles mountains hazy behind, late-afternoon light. **Le Morne**: the flat-topped Le Morne Brabant mountain rising over a turquoise lagoon, the One Eye reef break peeling on the outside, morning light.
