Shop conversations

Field sheet · six shops in Tamarin, La Preneuse and Black River · 5–13 October

Go with Abiguelle's introduction where she knows the shop, otherwise with Andras's intro message, and a sample bag if there is one. You are not selling today. You are learning what it would take for this shop to put the granola on a shelf after the Cup, and whether it is worth it for them and for us. Show the product and the price first. Mention the club only if they ask.

“We are making a small-batch granola in Tamarin. This is the bag and this is the shelf price. If a product like this sold well, what would you need from us to stock it? What margin do you work with? What is the smallest order you would take, and how do you want it delivered?”

“What they need” means anything they say before they would put it on a shelf: ingredient list, allergens, best-before, a barcode, an invoice, a health or hygiene certificate, a minimum shelf life, a returns rule. Write it down even if it sounds like a blocker.

Reading the results

A shop's margin sets the wholesale price. Put it into the granola sheet against the actual cost per bag before deciding anything. Two shops willing to take a small trial after the Cup is the target; one clear no with a reason is worth as much as a polite maybe. Pass the paperwork requirements to Andras the same day.
